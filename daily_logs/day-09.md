# Day 9 — Data Visualization and EDA

**Date:** 2026-09-04

## What I learned
- EDA helps understand data before machine learning modeling.
- Descriptive statistics summarize numeric features.
- Histograms show how values are distributed.
- Scatter plots help explore relationships between numeric variables.
- Correlation measures linear association and should not be interpreted as causation.

## What I practiced
- Loaded the study-progress CSV with Pandas.
- Inspected its shape and columns.
- Generated descriptive statistics and category counts.
- Calculated a numeric correlation matrix.

## Reflection
Today I learned that visualization and simple statistical summaries can reveal patterns and potential data issues before a model is trained. Good EDA makes later modeling decisions more informed.

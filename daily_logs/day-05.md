# Day 5 — Statistics for Machine Learning

**Date:** 2026-08-31

## What I learned
- Mean and median describe the center of a dataset.
- Variance and standard deviation describe how spread out values are.
- Range gives a simple measure from minimum to maximum.
- The mean can be strongly affected by outliers, while the median is usually more robust.
- Statistics help us understand data before building or evaluating ML models.

## What I practiced
- Loaded the existing study-progress CSV with Pandas.
- Converted the score column to a NumPy array.
- Calculated mean, median, variance, standard deviation, minimum, maximum, and range.
- Compared mean and median for symmetric and skewed examples.

## Experiment result
For the existing 8-row study-progress dataset, the mean score is **66.25** and the median score is **67.0**. The population standard deviation is approximately **15.77**.

These values describe the small practice dataset only and should not be generalized.

## Reflection
Today I understood why looking at only an average is not enough. Measures of spread and the shape of the data provide additional context and help identify when a statistic may be misleading.

# Day 7 — Distributions and Sampling

**Date:** 2026-09-02

## What I learned
- Distributions show how values are spread.
- Normal data is roughly symmetric, while skewed data has a longer tail.
- A population is the full group; a sample is a subset used to estimate it.
- Random samples vary, so sample statistics also vary.
- Repeated sampling helps us understand the stability of an estimate.

## What I practiced
- Generated a 10,000-value normal population with NumPy.
- Took samples of 100 values and repeated sampling 500 times.
- Compared population and sample means.
- Compared mean and median for symmetric and right-skewed data.

## Reflection
Today I understood why an ML dataset should be treated as a sample of a broader process. The shape of the data and the way a sample is selected both affect conclusions and generalization.

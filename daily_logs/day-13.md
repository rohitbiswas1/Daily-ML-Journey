# Day 13 — Classification Evaluation

**Date:** 2026-09-08

## What I learned
- Accuracy alone can hide poor performance on one class.
- Precision, recall, and F1 describe different error trade-offs.
- The confusion matrix makes false positives and false negatives visible.
- The probability threshold is a practical model-decision control.

## What I practiced
- Trained logistic regression on a small synthetic binary dataset.
- Evaluated thresholds at 0.30, 0.50, and 0.70.
- Compared accuracy, precision, recall, F1, and confusion matrices.

## Reflection
A classification model should be evaluated against the real cost of its mistakes, not just a single score.
